Scope & Purpose

A student-built, ever-expanding digital archive celebrating NASA 70 — seven decades of aerospace research from 1958 to 2028. It gathers the interactive projects produced by students of the Interaction Design course at SUPSI, starting with the first thirteen from 2026 and growing each year as the project will continue.
