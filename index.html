About The Archive

This ecosystem celebrates NASA's 70th anniversary by bringing together 13 interactive platforms developed in 2028. Unlike a traditional archive, the timeline is not based on publication dates, but rather maps the historical periods covered by the content. From pre-NASA antiquity and the discoveries of the space age, to infinite projections into the future.
